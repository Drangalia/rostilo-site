How can we help?

Email support@drangalia.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days. In the app, Settings → Help covers the most common questions.

I can't sign in

Use the same method you used when you created your account: Apple, Google or email. If you signed up with "Hide My Email", choose Continue with Apple again. For email accounts, passwords are case-sensitive, and new accounts must confirm their email first.

Restore my subscription

Open Rostilo, then tap Restore on the membership screen or Settings → Your account → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID that bought the subscription.

Cancel or change my subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rostilo. You can also get there from Settings → Your account → Manage subscription in the app. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.

Move to a new phone

Before switching, save a backup with Settings → Save a backup and restore it on your new phone with Settings → Restore a backup.

Import my rota

Settings → Import a rota accepts a screenshot of your rota, a CSV file or a calendar (ICS) file. You'll review every shift before anything is saved.

Delete my account

Go to Settings → Your account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and its data. It doesn't cancel your App Store subscription, so cancel that first.
