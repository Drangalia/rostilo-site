Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Rostilo is a shift work calendar for iPhone made by Drangalia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. In short: your schedule lives on your device, we don't run ads, we don't sell data, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1. Data stored on your device

Your shifts, events, templates, rotations, jobs, pay settings, notes and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone in the app's private storage. We cannot see this data.

  • Rota screenshots you choose to import are read with on-device text recognition. Photos and recognised text are not uploaded.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS after you turn them on.
  • The Home Screen widget receives a local copy of your upcoming shift names, times and job names.
  • Backups and exports (PDF, calendar files, images, backup files) are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your card details.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

We process account and subscription data to perform our contract with you. We process analytics and crash reports based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable and useful, and you can object at any time by turning analytics off. Where a law requires consent, we ask for it in the app.

4. Retention

Account and subscription records are kept while your account exists. Analytics events are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the app and are deleted with your account on request. When you delete your account, we delete your account, any cloud recovery data and the subscription record linked to your account ID. For Sign in with Apple accounts we also revoke the app's Apple tokens. Apple keeps its own purchase records under Apple's policies.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Your account → Delete my account.
  • Export your schedule at any time: Settings → Save a backup.
  • Turn off analytics: Settings → Privacy & your data.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, delete or port your data, or object to or restrict processing, by contacting us below. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Iceland, Persónuvernd).

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where data leaves the EEA or UK, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Children

Rostilo is a work tool and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.
